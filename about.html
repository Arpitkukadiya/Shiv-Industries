🌾 Who We Are

Our Story

Shiv Industries is a trusted name in Amreli, Gujarat, dedicated to manufacturing scientifically formulated cattle feed. We blend traditional knowledge with modern nutrition to empower dairy farmers. Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, affordable cattle feed that maximizes milk yield and ensures animal health.

Our Vision

To be India's most reliable cattle feed brand, driving dairy prosperity through high-quality nutrition and farmer-first innovation. We envision a future where every dairy farmer in India has access to premium nutrition that transforms their livelihood.

Our Mission

Customer satisfaction, uncompromised quality, and continuous R&D — ensuring every bag of feed boosts milk yield and animal health. We are committed to sustainable practices and empowering the farming community.

💎 Our Core Values

Quality First

We use only the finest raw materials and rigorous testing to ensure every batch meets the highest nutritional standards.

Farmer Partnership

We work closely with dairy farmers to understand their needs and provide tailored nutritional solutions.

Innovation

Continuous research and development to bring the latest advancements in animal nutrition to our farmers.

Animal Welfare

Our formulations are designed to promote optimal animal health, longevity, and well-being.
